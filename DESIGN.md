# Product Requirements Document (PRD) — SapiensStudying
**Status:** Approved / In Execution  
**Author:** AI Product Team & Design Lead  
**Date:** March 2026  
**Target Platform:** Web Desktop & Mobile Responsive  

---

## 1. Executive Summary & Vision

**SapiensStudying** is an AI-powered personal study planner designed to transform abstract learning intentions into actionable, science-backed study routines. Unlike traditional task managers, SapiensStudying uses pedagogical intelligence to sequence topics, balance cognitive load, curate multimodal learning resources (books, video courses, podcasts, articles), and enforce active retention practices (Feynman method, spaced repetition flashcards, interactive quizzes, and guided projects).

### 1.1 Core North Star
> *"Rooted Warmth & Methodical Depth"* — An approachable, organic digital environment that counters academic anxiety with clear progression, calm earthy aesthetics, and real-time AI guidance.

---

## 2. Target Audience & Problem Statement

### 2.1 Target Personas
1. **Self-Directed Tech & Career Learners (Primary):** Professionals and career switchers learning complex disciplines (Machine Learning, UX Design, Systems Architecture) without a formal syllabus.
2. **Academic & Competitive Exam Students:** Students preparing for certifications, university exams, or civil service tests needing rigorous schedule pacing.
3. **Lifelong Curious Learners:** Individuals exploring history, philosophy, or personal skills seeking curated paths rather than generic web search lists.

### 2.2 Core Problem
- **Information Overload:** Too many fragmented resources with zero clear sequencing.
- **Passive Learning Trap:** High consumption (watching videos, reading articles) with low active recall and retention.
- **Rigid Schedules:** Traditional planners break whenever life intervenes; students abandon the plan instead of recalibrating.

---

## 4. Detailed Feature Specifications

### 4.1 Plan Generator Engine (Home)
- **Input:** Natural language query (e.g., *"Machine Learning & Deep Learning"*).
- **Prompt Autocomplete & Trending Pills:** Quick selections for top disciplines.
- **Generation Output:**
  - Dynamic 4–12 week syllabus partitioned into weekly thematic milestones.
  - Daily activities (~45m to 2h) tagged with activity type (Reading, Practical Code/Exercise, Video, Spaced Review).
  - Pre-filtered curated library items (books, academic papers, courses).

### 4.2 Active Recall & Pedagogical Tools
- **Spaced Repetition Flashcards:** AI generates 15–50 adaptive cards per module.
- **Dynamic Diagnostic Quizzes:** 8-minute formative assessments validating conceptual grasp.
- **Feynman Technique Prompts:** Structured markdown template prompting users to explain concepts in plain language.
- **Hands-on Guided Mini-Projects:** Step-by-step milestones linking theory to practice.

### 4.3 Conversational AI Tutor ("Assistente Sapiens")
- **Accessibility:** Persistent slide-out drawer accessible from any study plan view.
- **Capabilities:**
  - One-click workload adjustments (*"Aumentar tempo diário"*, *"Mais exercícios práticos"*).
  - Context-aware conceptual tutoring explaining complex sub-topics.
  - Automatic schedule recalculation if days are missed.

### 4.4 Analytics & Intelligence Insights
- **Key Metrics:** Total hours studied, completion rate, daily active average, consecutive day streak.
- **Cognitive Diagnostics:** AI flags forgetting curves (*"5 days since linear algebra revision"*) and optimal peak performance windows (*"35% higher productivity during morning sessions"*).
- **Gamification:** Meaningful milestone badges (*Primeiros Passos, Foco Imparável, Mestre da Retenção*).

## 6. Success Metrics & KPIs (Product Evaluation)

1. **Activation Rate:** % of users who generate a study plan and complete Activity 1 on Day 1 (Target: >65%).
2. **Weekly Stickiness (W1 Retention):** % of users maintaining active study streaks across 7 consecutive days (Target: >35%).
3. **Plan Completion Rate:** Ratio of initiated modules to fully executed checklists (Target: >40%).
4. **AI Adaptation Rate:** % of users using the Sapiens Tutor to adjust workload or clarify concepts rather than abandoning.
